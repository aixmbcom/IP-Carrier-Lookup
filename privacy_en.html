🔒 Privacy Policy

We value and protect your privacy

Last Updated: January 2026

📋 Overview

Thank you for using the IP Geolocation Lookup Tool. We understand the importance of personal privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

📊 Information We Collect

Information collected:

  • IP Addresses: When you use our service to look up an IP, the address you enter is temporarily processed to provide results.
  • Visitor IP: When you click "My IP Address", we obtain your current public IP to provide the lookup service.
  • Technical Info: We may collect basic technical information such as browser type, operating system, and access time to improve service quality.

🎯 How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide IP geolocation lookup services
  • Improve and optimize user experience
  • Analyze service usage and enhance quality
  • Maintain website security and prevent abuse

🔐 Information Protection

We take the following measures to protect your information:

  • All queries are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS
  • We do not permanently store IP addresses you query
  • Query records are only used for temporary processing
  • We use industry-standard security measures

🌐 Third-Party Services

This tool uses third-party API services (IPinfo.io) to provide IP geolocation data. When you use our service:

  • Query requests are sent to third-party API servers
  • Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies
  • We recommend reviewing IPinfo.io's Privacy Policy

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use the following technologies:

  • Local Storage: For temporarily saving user preferences and query history (stored only in your browser)
  • Analytics: May use Google Analytics to analyze site traffic
  • Advertising: May use Google AdSense and other third-party ad services
  • You can manage or disable these via your browser settings

👤 Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Stop using our service at any time
  • Clear local storage data in your browser
  • Request information about how we use your data
  • Request deletion of any personal information we may hold

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. For significant changes, we will post a notice on the website. We recommend checking this page periodically.

📧 Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy:

  • Or visit our About page for more contact information
